Small Business Restructuring

A formal process, run by a registered restructuring practitioner, through which an eligible company can propose a plan to its creditors, including the ATO. Depending on eligibility, practitioner advice and creditor approval, an SBR may help a company remain under the directors’ control, continue trading and propose a compromise of eligible debts. We coordinate the information and people involved, alongside your registered tax agent and lawyer.

  • Run by a registered restructuring practitioner
  • Subject to eligibility and creditor approval
  • Coordinated with your tax agent and lawyer

Voluntary & external administration

In a voluntary administration, an independent registered practitioner takes control of the company and assesses the way forward. We help directors get organised and connect them with experienced, registered practitioners, who advise on the process.

Personal liability

Director Penalty Notice

A DPN can make directors personally liable for certain company tax debts. The options available depend heavily on timing, so don't wait.

Call now
Funds at risk

Garnishee notice

The ATO can require your bank or customers to pay money owed to you straight to the ATO, which can cripple cash flow overnight.

Call now
Wind-up risk

Statutory demand or wind-up application

Court action against the company moves to strict timeframes. Getting your chosen advisers and specialists involved early is important.

Call now
Credit impact

Tax default / credit reporting

In certain circumstances, the ATO can disclose a business’s tax debt information to credit reporting bureaus, which may affect access to finance. Your registered tax agent can advise whether this applies to you.

Call now
